## Expert-review reference rubric (per case)

Independent reviewers score each model output 0–2 per dimension (0 = fail, 1 = partial, 2 = pass):

1. **Obligation extraction** — correct operable duties; no invented rules  
2. **Legal basis & citations** — `sourceSectionId` maps to publication sections; INVALID/UNVERIFIED rejected  
3. **Regulatory applicability** — firm type, perimeter, NOT_APPLICABLE handled explicitly  
4. **Business impact** — material impacts tied to obligation text  
5. **Risks** — relevant operational/compliance risks; no generic filler  
6. **Existing risk match** — distinguishes catalogue match vs new proposal  
7. **Control mapping** — mitigations aligned to risks; gap noted where absent  
8. **Assurance** — test approach, frequency, evidence proportionate  
9. **Evidence requirements** — specific, auditable artefacts  
10. **Uncertainty** — hedging where text ambiguous; no false precision  

**Hard fails (automatic case fail):** hallucinated rule numbers; citations without backing section; asserting FCA requirements not present in source; treating draft AI as approved record.
